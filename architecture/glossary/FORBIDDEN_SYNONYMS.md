# Forbidden Synonyms

Version: 1.0 Draft

---

# Purpose

This document lists terminology that should not replace canonical SmartCoat terms.

---

# Rules

Use canonical terminology consistently.

Avoid synonyms that weaken architectural meaning.

---

# Forbidden or Deprecated Terms

## Industrial Intelligence

Use:

`Enterprise Intelligence`

## Engineering Intelligence

Use:

`Enterprise Intelligence`, unless specifically discussing engineering-domain reasoning.

## Industrial Knowledge

Use:

`Enterprise Knowledge`

## AI Platform

Use:

`Enterprise Intelligence Infrastructure`, unless specifically discussing the AI layer.

## Knowledge Platform

Use:

`Knowledge Architecture` or `Enterprise Intelligence Infrastructure`, depending on context.

## Decision Platform

Use:

`Decision Architecture` or `Enterprise Decision Intelligence`, depending on context.

## Chatbot

Use:

`Agent`, when referring to governed SmartCoat agents.

## Data Repository

Use:

`Knowledge Architecture`, `Data Platform`, or `Information Architecture`, depending on meaning.

---

# Forbidden Synonym Rule

If a term changes the architectural meaning, it must not be used as a synonym.
