# Canonical Terms

## Reality

The physical or business world independent of observation.

## Observation

A detected event, state, or phenomenon.

## Evidence

Verifiable support for an observation, claim, recommendation, or decision.

## Data

Raw representation of observations.

## Information

Structured data describing events.

## Knowledge

Understanding that explains information.

## Context

Relationships, dependencies, constraints, objectives, history, and meaning surrounding knowledge.

## Reasoning

The process of transforming contextual knowledge into possible actions or conclusions.

## Alternative

A feasible candidate action before a decision is made.

## Recommendation

A suggested course of action produced by reasoning.

## Decision

The selected course of action.

## Execution

The implementation of a decision.

## Outcome

The observed consequence of execution.

## Experience

The interpreted understanding of outcomes.

## Learning

Improvement of future behavior through experience.

## Organizational Capability

Accumulated ability of an enterprise to learn, adapt, decide, execute, and improve over time.

## Enterprise Knowledge

Connected understanding generated inside and around an organization that can improve future decisions and capability.

## Enterprise Intelligence

Organizational capability to transform enterprise knowledge into better enterprise decisions.

## Learning Enterprise

An organization whose capability continuously improves through enterprise learning.
