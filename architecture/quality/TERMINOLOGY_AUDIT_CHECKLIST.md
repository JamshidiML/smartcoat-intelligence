# Terminology Audit Checklist

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

This checklist supports terminology consistency review.

---

# Canonical Terms

Check consistent use of:

- Enterprise Knowledge
- Enterprise Context
- Enterprise Intelligence
- Enterprise Decisions
- Organizational Capability
- Learning Enterprise
- Knowledge Object
- Decision Object
- Evidence
- Provenance
- Context
- Reasoning
- Recommendation
- Alternative
- Execution
- Outcome
- Learning

---

# Deprecated Terms To Search

Search for:

- Industrial Intelligence
- Engineering Intelligence
- Industrial Knowledge
- AI Platform
- Knowledge Platform
- Decision Platform
- Chatbot, when referring to governed agents

---

# Audit Rule

Deprecated terms may remain only if used historically or with explicit clarification.
