Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

This chapter defines Decision Governance.

Decision Governance ensures that enterprise decisions are accountable, authorized, traceable, explainable, and aligned with organizational objectives.

---

# Governance Dimensions

- decision ownership
- approval authority
- evidence requirements
- risk thresholds
- confidence thresholds
- escalation rules
- audit requirements
- regulatory requirements
- security classification
- human accountability

---

# Governance Roles

- Decision Owner
- Domain Expert
- Approver
- Reviewer
- Compliance Officer
- Risk Owner
- Knowledge Steward
- System Agent

---

# Governance Rule

Decision authority must remain clear even when AI systems generate recommendations.
