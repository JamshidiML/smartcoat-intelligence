# Lifecycle Stages

## Problem Recognition

An enterprise situation requires a decision.

## Context Retrieval

Relevant enterprise knowledge and relationships are retrieved.

## Evidence Collection

Supporting observations, documents, events, and historical examples are gathered.

## Reasoning

The system and humans evaluate possible interpretations and actions.

## Alternative Generation

Multiple feasible actions are generated.

## Risk Evaluation

Risks, costs, constraints, uncertainties, and dependencies are assessed.

## Recommendation

A suggested action is proposed with reasoning and confidence.

## Decision

A responsible human or governance process selects an action.

## Execution

The selected decision is implemented.

## Outcome Observation

Results are measured and interpreted.

## Learning

The outcome updates enterprise knowledge and improves future decisions.

---

# Lifecycle Rule

A decision lifecycle is complete only after outcome and learning have been captured.
