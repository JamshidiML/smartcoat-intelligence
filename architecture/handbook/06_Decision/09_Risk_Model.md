# Purpose

This chapter defines risk within Decision Architecture.

Risk is a potential negative consequence that may affect a decision, execution, outcome, or organizational capability.

---

# Risk Categories

- Technical risk
- Quality risk
- Production risk
- Supplier risk
- Procurement risk
- Financial risk
- Regulatory risk
- Customer risk
- Safety risk
- Sustainability risk
- Strategic risk
- Knowledge risk

---

# Risk Attributes

Every risk object should include:

- risk_id
- risk_type
- description
- probability
- severity
- detectability
- affected_objects
- mitigation
- owner
- evidence
- confidence
- decision_impact

---

# Risk Principle

Risk is not only a safety or compliance concept.

Risk is a decision variable.

---

# Risk Rule

Every significant decision must identify relevant risks and mitigation options.
