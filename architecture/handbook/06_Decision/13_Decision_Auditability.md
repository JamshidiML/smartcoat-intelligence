# Purpose

This chapter defines Decision Auditability.

Decision Auditability enables organizations to reconstruct why a decision was made, which evidence supported it, who approved it, what alternatives existed, and what outcome occurred.

---

# Audit Requirements

Every significant decision should be auditable for:

- problem statement
- decision owner
- evidence
- context
- alternatives
- recommendation
- rationale
- assumptions
- risks
- confidence
- approvals
- execution
- outcome
- learning

---

# Why Auditability Matters

Auditability supports:

- trust
- regulatory compliance
- quality investigations
- customer communication
- internal learning
- AI accountability
- enterprise governance

---

# Auditability Rule

If a decision cannot be explained later, it was not captured properly.
