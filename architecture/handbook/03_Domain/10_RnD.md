# Purpose

This chapter defines the Research and Development Domain.

R&D represents projects, experiments, hypotheses, failures, lessons learned, customer-driven development, and scientific exploration.

---

# Core Entities

- R&D Project
- Hypothesis
- Experiment
- Trial
- Sample
- Test Plan
- Result
- Failure
- Success
- Lesson Learned
- Project Decision
- Customer Requirement
- Development Report
- Research Partner
- University Collaboration

---

# Key Relationships

- Project has Requirement
- Project contains Experiment
- Experiment tests Hypothesis
- Result supports Finding
- Failure generates Lesson Learned
- Decision modifies Project Direction
- Customer Requirement drives Development
- Research Partner contributes Knowledge

---

# Strategic Importance

R&D is one of the richest sources of enterprise knowledge.

Both successes and failures are strategic assets.

SmartCoat must preserve why projects succeeded, why they failed, and what should be reused in the future.

---

# Domain Rule

R&D output is not only a final product.

R&D output is reusable enterprise learning.
