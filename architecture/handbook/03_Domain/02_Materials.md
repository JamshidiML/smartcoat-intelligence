# Purpose

This chapter defines the Materials Domain.

The Materials Domain represents all raw materials, chemical ingredients, additives, fillers, polymers, coatings, textile substrates, and functional substances used within Advanced Materials organizations.

---

# Strategic Importance

Materials are one of the most important enterprise knowledge objects in SmartCoat.

A material decision can affect:

- product performance
- cost
- supply chain resilience
- regulatory compliance
- processing behavior
- quality stability
- customer approval
- environmental impact

---

# Domain Rule

No material shall be treated as a simple item number.

Every material is a knowledge object with technical, commercial, regulatory, logistical, and historical context.
