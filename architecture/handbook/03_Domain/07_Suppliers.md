Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

# Purpose

This chapter defines the Suppliers Domain.

The Suppliers Domain represents external organizations that provide materials, services, equipment, knowledge, logistics, testing, or manufacturing capacity.

---

# Core Entities

- Supplier
- Supplier Site
- Supplier Contact
- Material Offering
- Price
- Lead Time
- MOQ
- Capacity
- Availability
- Delivery Route
- Supplier Risk
- Technical Support
- Product Status
- Alternative Supplier
- Supplier Communication
- Supplier Evaluation

---

# Key Relationships

- Supplier provides Material
- Supplier has Lead Time
- Supplier has Price
- Supplier has Capacity
- Supplier has Region
- Supplier affects Logistics
- Supplier constrained by Regulation
- Supplier supports Substitution
- Supplier Communication creates Knowledge

---

# Strategic Importance

Supplier knowledge directly affects formulation decisions, production planning, cost, risk, and customer delivery.

Supply intelligence is therefore a core part of enterprise decision intelligence.

---

# Domain Rule

A supplier is not only a vendor.

A supplier is an enterprise knowledge source, risk source, and decision variable.
