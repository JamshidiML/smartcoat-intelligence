# Purpose

This chapter defines the domain architecture of SmartCoat.

Domain Architecture identifies the major industrial knowledge domains that SmartCoat must understand, connect, and reason across.

---

# Core Domain Thesis

Advanced Materials organizations do not make decisions inside isolated departments.

A single decision may depend on materials, formulations, suppliers, production capability, quality history, customer requirements, regulations, costs, logistics, market conditions, and previous experience.

Therefore, SmartCoat must model the enterprise as a connected domain system.

---

# Core Domains

SmartCoat recognizes the following initial domains:

- Materials
- Formulations
- Fabrics
- Manufacturing
- Quality Control
- Suppliers
- Customers
- Regulations
- Research and Development
- Projects
- Cost and Finance
- Logistics
- Market Intelligence
- Enterprise Decisions

---

# Domain Rule

Every domain must be modeled as a source of Enterprise Knowledge and Enterprise Context.

No domain is isolated.

Every domain contributes to decision intelligence.
