# Purpose

This chapter defines the Customers Domain.

The Customers Domain represents organizations, industries, applications, requirements, feedback, approvals, complaints, and strategic relationships.

---

# Core Entities

- Customer
- Customer Industry
- Application
- Requirement
- Specification
- Complaint
- Feedback
- Approval
- Customer Project
- Customer Test
- Customer Decision
- Customer Risk
- Customer Value

---

# Key Relationships

- Customer has Requirement
- Requirement constrains Product
- Customer tests Product
- Customer approves Formulation
- Customer Complaint triggers Quality Investigation
- Customer Industry implies Regulation
- Customer Feedback creates Knowledge

---

# Strategic Importance

Customer requirements define real-world product value.

SmartCoat must connect customer requirements to materials, formulations, production, quality, regulations, cost, risk, and decisions.

---

# Domain Rule

Customer knowledge must become part of enterprise intelligence, not remain isolated in sales communication.
