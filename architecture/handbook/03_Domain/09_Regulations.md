Version: 1.0

# Purpose

This chapter defines the Regulations Domain.

The Regulations Domain represents laws, standards, certifications, restrictions, environmental rules, safety requirements, and future regulatory risks.

---

# Core Entities

- Regulation
- Standard
- Certification
- Restricted Substance
- Region
- Compliance Requirement
- Test Standard
- Regulatory Risk
- Future Ban
- Documentation Requirement
- Approval Evidence

---

# Key Relationships

- Regulation constrains Material
- Regulation constrains Product
- Standard defines Test Method
- Certification applies to Product
- Region determines Compliance Requirement
- Restricted Substance affects Formulation
- Regulatory Risk affects Decision

---

# Strategic Importance

Regulatory knowledge must be connected to product design, materials, suppliers, customers, and markets.

Compliance by design requires regulatory intelligence to be present before decisions are made.

---

# Domain Rule

Regulation is not only documentation.

Regulation is a decision constraint.
