# Lifecycle Stages

## Capture

Knowledge is collected from people, systems, documents, agents, and events.

## Normalize

Knowledge is transformed into canonical formats.

## Classify

Knowledge is mapped to ontology and domain categories.

## Validate

Knowledge is reviewed for trust, evidence, and correctness.

## Connect

Knowledge is linked to enterprise objects and relationships.

## Enrich

Knowledge is enhanced with metadata, provenance, confidence, and context.

## Reuse

Knowledge supports search, reasoning, recommendations, and decisions.

## Evolve

Knowledge changes as new evidence, decisions, and outcomes appear.

---

# Lifecycle Rule

Knowledge must have lifecycle state and governance status before it influences high-impact decisions.
