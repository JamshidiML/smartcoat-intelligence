# Purpose

This chapter defines Enterprise Knowledge within the Knowledge Architecture.

---

# Definition

Enterprise Knowledge is the connected understanding generated inside and around an organization that can improve future decisions and organizational capability.

It includes technical, business, operational, financial, regulatory, supplier, customer, and market knowledge.

---

# Enterprise Knowledge Categories

- engineering knowledge
- production knowledge
- quality knowledge
- supplier knowledge
- customer knowledge
- financial knowledge
- market knowledge
- regulatory knowledge
- project knowledge
- strategic knowledge
- external knowledge

---

# Enterprise Knowledge Principle

Enterprise Knowledge must not remain trapped inside departments.

It must become reusable across the organization.

---

# Enterprise Knowledge Rule

Knowledge belongs to the enterprise, while ownership, access, confidentiality, and intellectual property must be governed.
