Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

This chapter defines Knowledge Governance.

Knowledge Governance ensures that enterprise knowledge remains trustworthy, secure, reusable, and aligned with enterprise objectives.

---

# Governance Areas

- ownership
- access control
- confidentiality
- intellectual property
- validation
- lifecycle management
- retention
- provenance
- auditability
- quality
- compliance
- change control

---

# Governance Roles

- Knowledge Owner
- Domain Owner
- Knowledge Steward
- Security Owner
- Compliance Owner
- Data Owner
- Architecture Owner

---

# Governance Rule

Knowledge that affects enterprise decisions must be governed.

Ungoverned knowledge may be useful for exploration but should not become authoritative.
