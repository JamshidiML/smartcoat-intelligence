Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

# Purpose

This chapter defines Tacit Knowledge Capture.

Tacit Knowledge is knowledge that exists inside human experience and is difficult to express in structured documents.

---

# Tacit Knowledge Examples

- operator intuition
- laboratory judgment
- supplier relationship knowledge
- troubleshooting experience
- formulation intuition
- production know-how
- customer-specific experience
- failure pattern recognition
- decision reasoning

---

# Capture Strategy

SmartCoat captures tacit knowledge through:

- agent interviews
- structured voice conversations
- guided questions
- project closure workflows
- production shift summaries
- quality investigation interviews
- supplier update interviews
- image-assisted explanation
- decision rationale capture

---

# Low-Friction Capture

Tacit knowledge capture must be easy.

The system should reduce writing effort and use natural conversation whenever possible.

---

# Tacit Knowledge Rule

If important knowledge exists only in a person's mind, it is an organizational risk.
