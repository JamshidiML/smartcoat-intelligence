# 09 Knowledge Validation

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

This chapter defines Knowledge Validation.

Knowledge Validation determines whether knowledge is trustworthy enough to support enterprise decisions.

---

# Validation Dimensions

- evidence quality
- source reliability
- provenance completeness
- domain expert review
- consistency with existing knowledge
- confidence level
- recency
- applicability
- regulatory sensitivity
- decision impact

---

# Validation States

- Draft
- Captured
- Reviewed
- Validated
- Approved
- Deprecated
- Rejected

---

# Validation Roles

- Knowledge Creator
- Domain Expert
- Knowledge Steward
- Decision Owner
- Compliance Reviewer
- Architecture Owner

---

# Validation Rule

High-impact decisions must not rely on unvalidated knowledge without explicit risk acknowledgement.
