# Purpose

This chapter defines Knowledge Capture within SmartCoat.

Knowledge Capture is the process of transforming enterprise experience into structured, reusable, and governed enterprise knowledge.

---

# Capture Philosophy

Knowledge must be captured as close as possible to the moment it is created.

The longer knowledge remains only in human memory or informal communication, the more context is lost.

---

# Capture Modes

SmartCoat supports multiple capture modes:

- structured forms
- agent interviews
- voice conversations
- document ingestion
- image ingestion
- email ingestion
- ERP extraction
- laboratory system integration
- production event capture
- supplier communication capture
- quality investigation capture
- manual expert annotation

---

# Capture Targets

Knowledge Capture should preserve:

- what happened
- why it happened
- who was involved
- what evidence exists
- what decision was made
- what alternatives were considered
- what risks existed
- what outcome occurred
- what should be learned

---

# Knowledge Capture Agents

SmartCoat agents should reduce the friction of knowledge capture.

They should ask structured questions, collect evidence, extract context, and generate knowledge objects without requiring employees to write long reports manually.

---

# Capture Rule

Knowledge capture must become part of the workflow.

It should not become a separate administrative burden.
