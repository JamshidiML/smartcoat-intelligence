# Purpose

This chapter defines the business model options for SmartCoat.

---

# Business Model Thesis

SmartCoat should not be limited to a single SaaS model.

The platform can support multiple business models because it connects enterprise knowledge, intelligence, and decisions.

---

# Business Model Layers

## Software Subscription

Enterprise software subscription for access to the SmartCoat platform.

## Data Intelligence Services

Specialized knowledge graph, ontology, data integration, and decision intelligence services.

## Industrial AI Modules

Modules for formulation intelligence, supplier intelligence, quality intelligence, and vision intelligence.

## Expert Implementation

Enterprise onboarding, domain modeling, knowledge migration, and integration services.

## IP-Protected Hybrid Manufacturing

Long-term optional model where SmartCoat controls formulation intelligence and specialized compound production while external partners perform toll manufacturing.

---

# Business Model Rule

Revenue models must not weaken the long-term knowledge moat.

Short-term services are acceptable only when they strengthen the Enterprise Intelligence Infrastructure.
