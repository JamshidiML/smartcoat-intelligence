# Purpose

This chapter defines the major business capabilities required by SmartCoat.

---

# Core Capabilities

## Knowledge Capture

Capture tacit and explicit enterprise knowledge.

## Knowledge Integration

Connect internal and external sources.

## Enterprise Context

Create relationships between knowledge objects.

## Decision Intelligence

Support explainable enterprise decisions.

## Supplier Intelligence

Understand suppliers, cost, logistics, availability, lead time, and risk.

## Materials Intelligence

Support materials, formulations, substitutions, and performance requirements.

## Quality Intelligence

Connect defects, tests, root causes, and lessons learned.

## Vision Intelligence

Analyze images and production defects.

## Regulatory Intelligence

Map materials, products, regions, and standards.

## Enterprise Agents

Embed knowledge capture and decision support into workflows.

---

# Capability Rule

A capability belongs to SmartCoat only if it improves knowledge, context, intelligence, decisions, or organizational capability.
