# Purpose

This chapter defines the initial beachhead strategy.

---

# Beachhead Market

Technical textiles and functional coatings are the first beachhead market.

This market is suitable because it combines:

- Complex materials
- Specialized formulations
- Production variability
- Supplier dependency
- Quality testing
- Regulatory constraints
- Customer-specific requirements
- High-value tacit knowledge

---

# Initial Use Case

The strongest initial use case is:

R&D and production knowledge capture for technical textile coating development.

---

# Expansion Path

1. R&D project memory
2. Formulation and material knowledge
3. Supplier and substitution intelligence
4. Production and quality intelligence
5. Computer vision defect intelligence
6. Enterprise decision intelligence

---

# Beachhead Rule

The first market must validate the full Enterprise Intelligence thesis in a narrow domain.
