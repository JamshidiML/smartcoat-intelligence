# Purpose

This chapter defines the market landscape in which SmartCoat operates.

SmartCoat is positioned at the intersection of Advanced Materials, Enterprise Intelligence, Knowledge Architecture, Decision Intelligence, and Industrial AI.

---

# Market Reality

Advanced Materials companies operate in highly knowledge-intensive environments.

Their decisions depend on:

- Materials science
- Formulation knowledge
- Production experience
- Supplier availability
- Quality history
- Regulatory constraints
- Customer requirements
- Cost and margin
- Logistics
- Sustainability
- Market volatility

Existing enterprise software manages many of these areas separately.

Few systems connect them into a continuously learning enterprise intelligence layer.

---

# Market Categories

SmartCoat touches several adjacent software and industrial technology categories:

- ERP
- PLM
- MES
- LIMS
- QMS
- CRM
- Business Intelligence
- Knowledge Management
- Materials Informatics
- Industrial AI
- Supply Chain Intelligence
- Decision Intelligence
- Knowledge Graph Platforms

SmartCoat does not replace these categories.

It connects and enriches them.

---

# Strategic Gap

The strategic gap is not the absence of software.

The gap is the absence of an enterprise intelligence layer specialized for Advanced Materials organizations.

This layer must connect enterprise knowledge, context, reasoning, and decisions across the entire organization.

---

# Market Landscape Rule

SmartCoat shall position itself as an intelligence layer above existing enterprise systems, not as a replacement for them.
