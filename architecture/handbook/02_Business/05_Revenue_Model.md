# Purpose

This chapter defines potential revenue models for SmartCoat.

---

# Revenue Streams

## Platform Subscription

Recurring enterprise subscription based on modules, users, sites, and data volume.

## Implementation Services

Paid enterprise onboarding, integration, ontology modeling, and knowledge migration.

## Intelligence Modules

Paid modules for specific capabilities:

- Formulation Intelligence
- Supplier Intelligence
- Decision Intelligence
- Quality Intelligence
- Computer Vision Intelligence
- Regulatory Intelligence

## Premium Knowledge Services

Curated external knowledge, supplier databases, standards mapping, and regulatory monitoring.

## Strategic Partnerships

Joint development with industrial partners, research institutions, and manufacturing networks.

---

# Revenue Principle

Revenue should grow with organizational value creation.

The strongest revenue model is one where SmartCoat becomes embedded in the customer's decision-making infrastructure.
