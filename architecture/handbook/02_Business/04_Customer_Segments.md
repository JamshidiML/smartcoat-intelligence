# Purpose

This chapter defines initial and future customer segments.

---

# Initial Customer Segment

The first beachhead customers are Advanced Materials companies with complex technical knowledge and fragmented enterprise systems.

Priority sectors:

- Technical textiles
- Functional coatings
- High-temperature insulation
- Polymer coating systems
- Specialty chemical coating suppliers
- Industrial textile manufacturers

---

# Buyer Personas

## Innovation Leader

Needs faster R&D and better knowledge reuse.

## R&D Manager

Needs access to previous projects, experiments, failures, and formulation reasoning.

## Production Manager

Needs production knowledge, defect history, and process context.

## Quality Manager

Needs connected test history, root causes, and traceability.

## Procurement Manager

Needs supplier alternatives, cost visibility, lead time, and risk intelligence.

## Executive Leader

Needs enterprise-level decision intelligence.

---

# Customer Segment Rule

SmartCoat should start with organizations where knowledge fragmentation creates measurable technical and economic pain.
