# Purpose

This chapter defines the strategic moat of SmartCoat.

---

# Core Moat

The long-term moat is not software.

It is accumulated enterprise knowledge, enterprise context, decision history, and organizational learning.

---

# Moat Components

## Domain Specialization

SmartCoat focuses on Advanced Materials instead of generic enterprise software.

## Knowledge Network

Value increases as more enterprise knowledge becomes connected.

## Decision History

Every decision improves future recommendations and reasoning.

## Ontology

The semantic model becomes a proprietary architecture asset.

## Agent Workflows

Knowledge capture becomes embedded into daily work.

## Supply Intelligence

Supplier, logistics, cost, lead-time, and substitution intelligence create practical business value.

---

# Competitive Rule

SmartCoat should compete through accumulated intelligence, not feature volume.
