# Purpose

This chapter defines the product strategy of SmartCoat.

---

# Product Strategy

SmartCoat should evolve from focused intelligence modules into a full Enterprise Intelligence Infrastructure.

---

# Product Phases

## Phase 1 — Industrial Memory

Capture knowledge from projects, documents, voice, images, and enterprise systems.

## Phase 2 — Knowledge Network

Connect materials, suppliers, projects, experiments, tests, customers, costs, and decisions.

## Phase 3 — Decision Intelligence

Generate explainable recommendations and decision support.

## Phase 4 — Enterprise Agents

Deploy specialized agents for lab, production, quality, supplier, warehouse, and executive workflows.

## Phase 5 — Learning Enterprise

Enable continuous organizational learning across the full enterprise.

---

# Product Rule

Every product must strengthen the Enterprise Intelligence Model.
