# Purpose

This chapter defines major business risks.

---

# Risk Categories

## Scope Risk

SmartCoat can become too broad if it tries to serve every enterprise function too early.

## Data Access Risk

Enterprise intelligence depends on access to high-quality data and knowledge.

## Adoption Risk

Employees may resist knowledge capture if workflows are difficult.

## Integration Risk

Enterprise systems are complex and heterogeneous.

## Trust Risk

Recommendations must be explainable and auditable.

## Market Positioning Risk

SmartCoat must avoid being perceived as a generic AI chatbot or ERP replacement.

---

# Risk Mitigation

Start with narrow pilots.

Prioritize explainability.

Integrate with existing systems.

Make knowledge capture low-friction.

Focus on Advanced Materials.

Build trust through evidence.

---

# Risk Rule

Every risk must be evaluated against long-term organizational capability.
