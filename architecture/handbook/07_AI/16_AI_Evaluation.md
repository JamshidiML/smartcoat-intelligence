Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

This chapter defines AI evaluation in SmartCoat.

Evaluation determines whether AI capabilities are reliable enough to support enterprise workflows and decisions.

---

# Evaluation Categories

## Retrieval Evaluation

- relevance
- precision
- recall
- source quality
- context completeness

## Generation Evaluation

- factuality
- groundedness
- clarity
- completeness
- hallucination rate

## Prediction Evaluation

- accuracy
- precision
- recall
- F1 score
- calibration
- robustness

## Recommendation Evaluation

- decision usefulness
- explainability
- confidence quality
- risk awareness
- expert acceptance

## Vision Evaluation

- detection accuracy
- segmentation quality
- false positives
- false negatives
- class imbalance
- production robustness

---

# Evaluation Rule

AI success must be measured by decision improvement, not only model performance.
