# 03 AI Operating Principles

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

This chapter defines operating principles for AI systems inside SmartCoat.

---

# Principles

## AI Must Be Contextual

AI outputs must be grounded in enterprise context.

## AI Must Be Traceable

AI recommendations must reference sources, evidence, and reasoning.

## AI Must Be Governed

AI systems must follow access, security, privacy, IP, and compliance rules.

## AI Must Be Evaluated

Every AI capability must have evaluation metrics.

## AI Must Be Explainable

High-impact recommendations require human-understandable explanations.

## AI Must Be Domain-Aware

AI systems must align with domain architecture and enterprise language.

## AI Must Learn From Outcomes

Decision outcomes should improve future AI behavior.

---

# Operating Rule

AI without context, evidence, and governance is not allowed to influence high-impact enterprise decisions.
