# 09 Regulatory Intelligence

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

This chapter defines Regulatory Intelligence.

Regulatory Intelligence connects materials, products, customers, regions, standards, and regulations to support compliance-aware decisions.

---

# Core Capabilities

- regulation mapping
- restricted substance screening
- regional compliance checking
- future restriction monitoring
- standard-to-test mapping
- certification evidence retrieval
- regulatory risk scoring
- compliance-by-design support

---

# Inputs

- regulations
- standards
- material composition
- SDS
- product applications
- customer regions
- certification history
- test methods
- regulatory updates

---

# Outputs

- compliance status
- risk warnings
- restricted substance alerts
- required tests
- documentation requirements
- regulatory decision support

---

# Rule

Regulatory Intelligence must be integrated before product decisions, not after development is complete.
