# 15 AI Safety And Trust

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

This chapter defines AI safety and trust principles for SmartCoat.

---

# Trust Requirements

AI systems must be:

- explainable
- traceable
- evidence-based
- secure
- access-controlled
- monitored
- human-reviewable
- domain-aware
- uncertainty-aware
- aligned with decision governance

---

# Safety Concerns

- hallucination
- incorrect recommendation
- hidden bias
- outdated knowledge
- missing context
- unauthorized access
- IP leakage
- regulatory error
- overreliance
- model drift

---

# Mitigation

- source grounding
- confidence scoring
- human approval
- audit logs
- access control
- validation workflows
- monitoring
- rollback procedures
- model cards
- decision impact classification

---

# Safety Rule

AI should never create the illusion of certainty when uncertainty exists.
