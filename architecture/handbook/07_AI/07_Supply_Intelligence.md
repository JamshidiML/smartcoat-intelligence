# 07 Supply Intelligence

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

This chapter defines Supply Intelligence.

Supply Intelligence supports supplier, logistics, availability, price, risk, and substitution decisions.

---

# Core Capabilities

- supplier search
- supplier comparison
- availability monitoring
- lead time prediction
- price trend analysis
- MOQ analysis
- logistics risk assessment
- geographic optimization
- supplier substitution
- supplier reliability scoring

---

# Inputs

- supplier data
- pricing
- lead time
- MOQ
- capacity
- location
- transport routes
- weather conditions
- geopolitical risk
- material compatibility
- historical supplier performance

---

# Outputs

- supplier recommendations
- risk alerts
- cost forecasts
- lead time estimates
- alternative supply plans
- sourcing decisions

---

# Rule

Supply Intelligence must connect technical suitability with business, logistics, cost, and risk context.
