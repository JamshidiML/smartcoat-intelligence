# 05 Materials Intelligence

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

This chapter defines Materials Intelligence.

Materials Intelligence uses enterprise knowledge, materials data, supplier information, regulations, performance history, cost, logistics, and decision context to support better material decisions.

---

# Core Capabilities

- material search
- material comparison
- substitution recommendation
- performance similarity
- regulatory screening
- supplier availability analysis
- cost-performance analysis
- storage and shelf-life analysis
- material risk analysis

---

# Inputs

- material specifications
- TDS
- SDS
- supplier data
- pricing
- availability
- formulation history
- test results
- regulations
- customer requirements
- production experience

---

# Outputs

- candidate materials
- alternatives
- risk assessment
- cost-performance trade-offs
- regulatory warnings
- confidence levels
- decision recommendations

---

# Rule

Materials Intelligence must never recommend a material without considering context, constraints, evidence, and decision impact.
