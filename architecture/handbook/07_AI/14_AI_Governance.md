# Purpose

This chapter defines AI Governance.

AI Governance ensures that AI systems are trustworthy, secure, explainable, auditable, and aligned with enterprise objectives.

---

# Governance Areas

- model ownership
- data ownership
- access control
- model validation
- evaluation metrics
- monitoring
- drift detection
- bias assessment
- explainability
- human review
- regulatory compliance
- incident response
- change management

---

# Governance Roles

- AI Owner
- Model Owner
- Data Owner
- Domain Expert
- Security Owner
- Compliance Owner
- Decision Owner
- Architecture Owner

---

# Governance Rule

AI systems that influence high-impact decisions must be governed, monitored, and auditable.
