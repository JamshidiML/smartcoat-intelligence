# Purpose

This chapter defines the strategic role of AI in SmartCoat.

---

# AI Strategy Statement

SmartCoat uses AI to transform enterprise knowledge into explainable, contextual, and continuously improving enterprise decisions.

AI is applied where it improves:

- knowledge capture
- knowledge extraction
- context retrieval
- reasoning
- prediction
- recommendation
- optimization
- explainability
- learning

---

# Strategic Position

SmartCoat does not compete as a generic AI chatbot.

SmartCoat applies AI inside a governed Enterprise Intelligence Architecture specialized for Advanced Materials industries.

---

# AI Strategy Principles

## Domain First

AI must respect domain meaning.

## Knowledge First

AI must use governed enterprise knowledge.

## Context First

AI recommendations must include context.

## Human Accountable

Humans remain responsible for high-impact decisions.

## Explainability Required

AI must explain recommendations when decisions are important.

## Continuous Learning

AI systems must improve through outcomes and feedback.

---

# AI Strategy Rule

AI should be introduced only where it strengthens Enterprise Intelligence and decision quality.
