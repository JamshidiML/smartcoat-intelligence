# Purpose

This chapter defines the AI Architecture of SmartCoat.

AI Architecture describes how artificial intelligence capabilities support Enterprise Intelligence across knowledge, context, reasoning, decisions, and learning.

---

# Core Thesis

Artificial Intelligence does not replace Enterprise Intelligence.

Artificial Intelligence supports Enterprise Intelligence.

AI becomes valuable only when it improves enterprise decisions, strengthens organizational learning, or increases organizational capability.

---

# AI Architecture Layers

SmartCoat AI Architecture consists of the following layers:

1. Knowledge Retrieval
2. Context Construction
3. Reasoning Support
4. Prediction
5. Recommendation
6. Optimization
7. Explanation
8. Learning Feedback
9. Governance

---

# AI Role

AI should support:

- semantic search
- document understanding
- knowledge extraction
- image analysis
- material recommendation
- supplier analysis
- risk prediction
- decision support
- agent workflows
- continuous learning

---

# AI Architecture Rule

No AI capability shall operate outside the Enterprise Ontology, Canonical Information Model, Knowledge Architecture, and Decision Architecture.
