# Purpose

This chapter defines the Master Data Strategy of SmartCoat.

---

# Master Data Domains

Initial master data domains include:

- Materials
- Suppliers
- Customers
- Products
- Formulations
- Fabrics
- Machines
- Test Methods
- Standards
- Regulations
- Locations
- Persons
- Organizations

---

# Master Data Principle

Master data must provide trusted identity for core enterprise objects.

Without identity, relationships cannot be reliable.

Without relationships, context cannot be reliable.

Without context, decisions cannot be reliable.

---

# Master Data Rule

Every critical enterprise object must have a canonical identity before it can participate in decision intelligence.
