---

# Purpose

This chapter defines how SmartCoat evaluates data quality.

---

# Data Quality Dimensions

- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Consistency
- Timeliness
- Validity
- Uniqueness
- Provenance
- Relevance
- Confidence
- Usability for decisions

---

# Quality Principle

Data quality is not an abstract technical property.

Data quality is measured by whether information is reliable enough to support enterprise decisions.

---

# Data Quality Rule

Data that cannot support reasoning, context, learning, or decision-making must be improved before becoming part of Enterprise Intelligence workflows.
