# Purpose

This chapter defines the Provenance Model of SmartCoat.

Provenance explains where knowledge came from, who created it, when it was created, how it was changed, and why it can be trusted.

---

# Provenance Requirements

Every important object should include:

- source system
- source document
- creator
- timestamp
- method of creation
- evidence
- transformation history
- validation status
- confidence
- reviewer
- approval state

---

# Provenance Importance

Provenance is required for:

- trust
- auditability
- explainability
- regulatory compliance
- AI confidence
- decision traceability
- knowledge validation

---

# Provenance Rule

No recommendation should be trusted unless its supporting knowledge has provenance.
