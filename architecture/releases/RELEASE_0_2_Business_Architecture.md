# Release 0.2 — Business Architecture

Version: 0.2

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

This release introduces the Business Architecture layer of SmartCoat.

It defines how SmartCoat creates value, which markets it serves, which capabilities it requires, and how the business strategy connects to the Enterprise Intelligence Architecture.

---

# Scope

This release includes:

- Business Architecture overview
- Market landscape
- Value proposition
- Business model
- Customer segments
- Revenue model
- Competitive advantage
- Go-to-market strategy
- Product strategy
- Capability map
- Beachhead market strategy
- Business risk model

---

# Architectural Rule

Business Architecture must derive from the Foundation and Reference Models.

No business capability shall be introduced unless it strengthens Enterprise Knowledge, Enterprise Context, Enterprise Intelligence, Enterprise Decisions, or Organizational Capability.
