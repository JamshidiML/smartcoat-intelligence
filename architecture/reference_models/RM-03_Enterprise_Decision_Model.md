# RM-03 Enterprise Decision Model

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

This reference model defines how SmartCoat understands enterprise decisions.

The purpose of Enterprise Intelligence is to improve enterprise decisions.

---

# Core Decision Model

Knowledge

↓

Context

↓

Reasoning

↓

Alternatives

↓

Decision

↓

Execution

↓

Outcome

↓

Learning

↓

Better Future Decisions

---

# Decision Types

SmartCoat supports multiple decision categories:

- Operational decisions
- Tactical decisions
- Strategic decisions
- Engineering decisions
- Production decisions
- Supplier decisions
- Procurement decisions
- Quality decisions
- Financial decisions
- Customer decisions
- Executive decisions

---

# Decision Quality

Decision quality depends on:

- Evidence
- Context
- Alternatives
- Risk
- Cost
- Constraints
- Confidence
- Explainability
- Execution feedback

---

# Rule

Every SmartCoat capability shall ultimately improve decision quality, decision speed, decision confidence, decision explainability, or organizational learning.
