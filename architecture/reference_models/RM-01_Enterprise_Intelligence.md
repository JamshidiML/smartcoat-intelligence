# RM-01 Enterprise Intelligence Reference Model

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# Purpose

This reference model defines Enterprise Intelligence as the central operating model of SmartCoat.

All SmartCoat architecture, software, data models, AI systems, agents, knowledge graphs, and decision systems shall be derived from this model.

---

# Definition

Enterprise Intelligence is the organizational capability to continuously transform enterprise knowledge into better enterprise decisions and long-term organizational capability.

It is not the same as Artificial Intelligence.

Artificial Intelligence is an implementation capability.

Enterprise Intelligence is an organizational capability.

---

# Core Model

Enterprise Knowledge

↓

Enterprise Context

↓

Enterprise Intelligence

↓

Enterprise Decisions

↓

Organizational Capability

↓

Learning Enterprise

---

# Layer 1 — Enterprise Knowledge

Enterprise Knowledge includes all knowledge generated inside and around an organization.

Examples:

- Engineering
- Production
- Quality
- Finance
- Sales
- Procurement
- Supply Chain
- Customers
- Suppliers
- Regulations
- Projects
- Scientific literature
- Market intelligence

---

# Layer 2 — Enterprise Context

Enterprise Context connects knowledge through relationships.

Examples:

- Material to supplier
- Supplier to price
- Price to formulation
- Formulation to product
- Product to customer
- Customer to requirement
- Requirement to regulation
- Regulation to decision

Context transforms isolated knowledge into usable intelligence.

---

# Layer 3 — Enterprise Intelligence

Enterprise Intelligence reasons across connected knowledge.

It combines:

- Domain expertise
- Scientific reasoning
- Business reasoning
- AI
- Optimization
- Simulation
- Search
- Recommendation
- Risk analysis

---

# Layer 4 — Enterprise Decisions

Enterprise Intelligence exists to improve decisions.

Examples:

- Engineering decisions
- Production decisions
- Supplier decisions
- Procurement decisions
- Quality decisions
- Financial decisions
- Strategic decisions

---

# Layer 5 — Organizational Capability

Better decisions improve organizational capability.

Capability includes:

- Faster learning
- Better knowledge retention
- Lower risk
- Better innovation
- Stronger supply resilience
- Better decision quality

---

# Rule

Every SmartCoat capability must strengthen at least one layer of the Enterprise Intelligence Model.

If it does not improve knowledge, context, intelligence, decision-making, or organizational capability, it does not belong to the SmartCoat core architecture.
