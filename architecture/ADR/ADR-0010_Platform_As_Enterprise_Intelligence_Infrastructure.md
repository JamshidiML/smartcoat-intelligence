# ADR-0010 Platform As Enterprise Intelligence Infrastructure

Status: Accepted

---

# Context

SmartCoat requires software, data, AI, agent, integration, graph, and governance capabilities.

Without a platform architecture, these components may evolve as disconnected tools.

---

# Decision

SmartCoat shall be implemented as a modular Enterprise Intelligence Platform.

The platform shall support knowledge, context, AI, agents, decisions, integrations, governance, security, and observability through coherent services and canonical architecture.

---

# Consequences

- Services must align with domain and reference models.
- APIs must use canonical enterprise language.
- Agents must operate through governed platform services.
- AI systems must retrieve context through approved platform layers.
- Knowledge Graph and data platform must remain aligned.
- Security and governance must be platform-native.
