# ADR-0008 AI As Enterprise Intelligence Capability

Status: Accepted

---

# Context

SmartCoat uses AI extensively, but AI is not the identity of the platform.

Without this distinction, the architecture risks becoming model-centric rather than knowledge-, context-, and decision-centric.

---

# Decision

Artificial Intelligence shall be treated as an implementation capability of Enterprise Intelligence, not as the core identity of SmartCoat.

---

# Consequences

- AI systems must align with Foundation, Reference Models, Domain Architecture, Information Architecture, Knowledge Architecture, and Decision Architecture.
- AI capabilities must support enterprise decisions and organizational learning.
- AI outputs must be explainable, contextual, governed, and auditable according to decision impact.
