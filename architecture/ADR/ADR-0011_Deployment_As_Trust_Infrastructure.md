# ADR-0011 Deployment As Trust Infrastructure

Status: Accepted

---

# Context

SmartCoat manages enterprise knowledge, AI agents, decision support, and sensitive industrial information.

Deployment choices directly affect customer trust, knowledge security, reliability, and operational adoption.

---

# Decision

SmartCoat shall treat deployment architecture as trust infrastructure.

Deployment decisions must prioritize security, governance, reliability, observability, backup, recovery, and customer constraints.

---

# Consequences

- Deployment models must support cloud, on-premise, and hybrid options.
- Production deployments require monitoring, backup, and rollback.
- Agent and AI deployment must be governed.
- Customer trust is a deployment architecture requirement, not only a business concern.
