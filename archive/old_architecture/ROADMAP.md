# SmartCoat Roadmap
SmartCoat is an Industrial Decision Intelligence Platform that optimizes materials, manufacturing, supply chains, regulations, logistics, climate adaptation, and formulation decisions simultaneously.

## Phase 0 (2026)

Knowledge Foundation

Objective:
Capture and structure industrial knowledge.

Deliverables:

* Enterprise Ontology
* Knowledge Objects
* Knowledge Graph
* PostgreSQL Foundation
* Document Intelligence

---

## Phase 1 (2026-2027)

Materials Intelligence

Objective:
Build the world's largest technical textile and coating materials intelligence database.

Capabilities:

* Material Library
* Supplier Library
* Alternative Materials
* Performance Database
* Shelf-Life Database
