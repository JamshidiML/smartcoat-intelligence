# SmartCoat Enterprise Architecture

## Architecture Philosophy

SmartCoat is not a formulation software.

SmartCoat is an Industrial Decision Intelligence Platform.

---

# Layer 1

Knowledge Foundation

Purpose:

Create a unified industrial knowledge repository.

Components:

* Documents
* Projects
* Materials
* Fabrics
* Standards
* Tests
* Defects
* Lessons Learned

---

# Layer 2

Materials Intelligence

Purpose:

Understand materials and their alternatives.

Capabilities:

* Material substitution
* Cost-performance analysis
* Availability analysis
* Shelf-life optimization

---

# Layer 3

Manufacturing Intelligence

Purpose:

Capture and optimize production knowledge.

Capabilities:

* Process optimization
* Defect intelligence
* Batch intelligence
* Yield optimization

---

# Layer 4

Supply Chain Intelligence

Purpose:

Optimize sourcing and logistics.

Capabilities:

* Supplier intelligence
* Route optimization
* Lead-time forecasting
* Inventory optimization

---

# Layer 5

Regulatory & Climate Intelligence

Purpose:

Ensure compliance and environmental suitability.

Capabilities:

* REACH analysis
* FDA analysis
* EPA analysis
* Climate adaptation
* Future restriction forecasting

---

# Layer 6

Decision Intelligence

Purpose:

Generate actionable industrial decisions.

Outputs:

* Recommended formulation
* Recommended suppliers
* Cost forecast
* Risk assessment
* Production schedule
* Procurement strategy

---

# Layer 7

AI Systems

Purpose:

Provide predictive and generative intelligence.

Components:

* Project Success Predictor
* Defect Predictor
* Market Forecasting Engine
* Supply-Aware Formulation Engine
* Autonomous Industrial Agents

---

# Layer 8

Autonomous Industrial Operating System

Purpose:

Enable continuous industrial optimization.

The system continuously learns from:

* Manufacturing
* Supply Chains
* Markets
* Regulations
* Climate
* Scientific Research

and autonomously improves industrial decision quality.
