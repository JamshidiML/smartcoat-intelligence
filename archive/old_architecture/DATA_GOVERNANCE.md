# SmartCoat Data Governance

## Principle 1

Data is an asset.

Industrial knowledge is the core asset of SmartCoat.

---

## Principle 2

Every record must have:

* Source
* Timestamp
* Owner
* Confidence Score
* Version

---

## Principle 3

No data enters the platform without metadata.

---

## Principle 4

Raw data is immutable.

Raw files are never modified.

---

## Principle 5

Every transformation must be traceable.

---

## Principle 6

Knowledge lineage must be preserved.

The platform must always be able to answer:

Where did this information come from?

---

## Principle 7

Confidentiality Levels

Public

Internal

Confidential

Restricted

Strategic

---

## Principle 8

Industrial Knowledge First

The platform stores:

* R&D knowledge
* Failure knowledge
* Manufacturing knowledge
* Supply chain knowledge
* Regulatory knowledge
* Market knowledge

as first-class assets.

---

## Principle 9

AI Never Overrides Facts

AI generates recommendations.

Human-reviewed knowledge remains authoritative.

---

## Principle 10

Every decision must be explainable.
